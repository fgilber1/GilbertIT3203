HTTP: The WorldWide Language of the Web

HyperText Transfer Protocol(better known as HTTP) is the foundation of the World Wide Web. This application-level protocol is designed to transfer/communicate information between web clients and servers.

Introduction

The purpose of this website is to provide in-depth information on the Hypertext Transfer Protocol for those who aim to have a deeper understanding of HTTP.
